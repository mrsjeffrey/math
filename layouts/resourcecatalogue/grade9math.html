Strand AA: Social-Emotional Learning (SEL)

AA1 - Social-Emotional Learning Skills
Develop and explore a variety of social-emotional learning skills in a context that supports and reflects this learning in connection with the expectations across all other strand.
AA1.1 – Use self-awareness, self-management, social awareness, relationship skills, and responsible decision-making to support learning and problem solving in mathematics.
↑ ↓

Strand A: Mathematical Thinking and Making Connections

A1 - Mathematical Processes
Apply the mathematical processes to develop a conceptual understanding of, and procedural fluency with, the mathematics they are learning.
A1.1 – Identify real-life situations that can be represented mathematically and describe the variables and assumptions involved.
A1.2 – Create and refine mathematical models to represent real-life situations using appropriate tools, representations, and strategies.
A1.3 – Analyse mathematical models, assess their effectiveness, and make improvements where necessary.
A1.4 – Communicate conclusions drawn from mathematical models and describe their limitations.
A2 - Making Connections
Make connections between mathematics and various knowledge systems, their lived experiences, and various real-life applications of mathematics, including careers.
A2.1 – Use mathematical reasoning to make and justify conjectures.
A2.2 – Identify and explain errors or misconceptions in mathematical reasoning.
A2.3 – Evaluate strategies and solutions for efficiency, accuracy, and clarity.
↑ ↓

Strand B: Number

B1 - Development of Numbers and Number Sets
Demonstrate an understanding of numbers and apply this understanding in various contexts.
B1.1 – Represent and compare numbers, including integers, fractions, decimals, and percents, using a variety of tools and representations.
B1.2 – Solve problems involving ratios, rates, and unit rates using proportional reasoning.
B1.3 – Apply proportional reasoning in real-life contexts, including scale drawings and financial situations.
B2 - Powers
represent numbers in various ways, evaluate powers, and simplify expressions by using the relationships between powers and their exponent.
B2.1 – Represent powers with whole-number exponents and describe their meaning.
B2.2 – Apply exponent rules to simplify numerical expressions involving powers.
B2.3 – Use powers of ten and scientific notation to represent and compare large and small numbers.
↑ ↓

Strand C: Algebra

C1 - Algebraic Expressions and Equations
Represent and analyze mathematical relationships using algebraic expressions, equations, graphs, and coding.
C1.1 – Translate between verbal descriptions, algebraic expressions, and equations.
C1.2 – Simplify algebraic expressions involving variables, integers, and whole-number coefficients.
C1.3 – Evaluate algebraic expressions by substituting given values for variables.
C2 - Linear Relations
Represent, compare, and analyze linear and non-linear relations using various representations.
C2.1 – Represent linear relations using tables, graphs, equations, and verbal descriptions.
C2.2 – Determine and interpret the rate of change from various representations of linear relations.
C2.3 – Graph linear relations and interpret key features such as slope and intercepts.
C2.4 – Compare linear and non-linear relations using graphs, tables, and equations.
C3 - Coding
Solve problems and model mathematical relationships using coding.
C3.1 – Write and modify code involving variables, loops, and conditionals to model mathematical situations.
C3.2 – Use coding to solve problems and represent mathematical relationships.
C3.3 – Predict, test, and explain the output of code used to represent mathematical ideas.
↑ ↓

Strand D: Data

D1 – Data Collection and Representation
Collect, organize, represent, and analyze data to draw conclusions and make informed decisions.
D1.1 – Collect and organize data from primary and secondary sources using appropriate methods and tools.
D1.2 – Represent data using charts, tables, and graphs, selecting formats that best suit the data and context.
D1.3 – Interpret data representations to identify trends, patterns, and relationships.
D2 – Data Analysis
Analyze data using statistical measures and evaluate conclusions.
D2.1 – Calculate and interpret measures of central tendency to summarize data sets.
D2.2 – Compare data sets using measures of central tendency and graphical representations.
D2.3 – Draw conclusions from data analysis and assess the reasonableness of those conclusions.
↑ ↓

Strand E: Geometry and Measurement

E1 – Geometric Relationships
Identify and analyze geometric relationships using mathematical reasoning.
E1.1 – Identify and describe geometric relationships involving angles, triangles, and polygons.
E1.2 – Apply properties of angles and shapes to solve problems involving geometric figures.
E1.3 – Use geometric reasoning to justify conclusions and explain relationships.
E2 – Measurement
Solve problems involving measurement using appropriate tools, formulas, and strategies.
E2.1 – Solve problems involving perimeter, area, surface area, and volume of various shapes.
E2.2 – Apply measurement concepts to real-life situations, including scale and unit conversions.
E2.3 – Use appropriate tools and formulas to measure and calculate geometric quantities accurately.
↑

Strand F: Financial Literacy

F1 – Financial Decision Making
Demonstrate an understanding of financial concepts and apply mathematical reasoning to make informed financial decisions.
F1.1 – Identify various sources of income and explain factors that affect earnings.
F1.2 – Create and analyze simple budgets to plan for short-term and long-term financial goals.
F1.3 – Compare different methods of payment and explain their advantages and disadvantages.
F1.4 – Calculate costs involving taxes, discounts, tips, and simple interest.
F1.5 – Describe how financial decisions can impact personal well-being and future opportunities.
